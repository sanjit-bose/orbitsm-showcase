# Product Features

## Incident Management

- Incident creation, triage, assignment, prioritization, and lifecycle tracking.
- SLA due dates, breach indicators, and operational pressure views.
- Comments, attachments, ownership, and status updates.
- AI-assisted RCA, similarity search, and categorization support.
- Linkage into problem and change governance.

## Task Management

- Separate task queue from incidents and service requests.
- Project-aware task visibility.
- Assignment, status transitions, due dates, and SLA context.
- Kanban participation alongside other work items.

## Service Request Management

- Request intake and fulfillment tracking.
- Requester visibility and assignment controls.
- Lifecycle movement from open to fulfillment/closure.
- Project and customer context.

## Problem Management

- Problem records for recurring or high-impact incident clusters.
- RCA capture and maturity tracking.
- Known error and workaround support.
- Permanent fix planning.
- Downstream change linkage.

## Change Management

- Change request tracking.
- Risk and impact context.
- L1/CAB approval stages.
- Scheduling and implementation status.
- Rollback and closure governance.

## Project and Customer Governance

- Admin-created projects.
- Project code and SLA configuration.
- Team member assignment.
- Customer contact assignment.
- User access restricted by project, role, hierarchy, and customer/account context.

## Analytics and Reporting

- SLA compliance.
- MTTR.
- Open/closed work item counts.
- Problem and change health.
- Relationship coverage.
- AI insights.
- Workload and exception reporting.

## AI-Assisted Knowledge

- AI-generated RCA summaries.
- Similar incident recommendations.
- KEDB and KB article drafting.
- Download-ready knowledge artifacts.
- AI signals for operational dashboards.
