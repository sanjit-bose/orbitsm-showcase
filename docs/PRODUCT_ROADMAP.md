# Product Roadmap

This roadmap presents the product vision at a public-safe level. It does not disclose implementation plans that would expose proprietary intellectual property.

## Near-Term Enhancements

- Expand AI copilot workflows for service desk, problem management, and change governance.
- Improve executive dashboards for SLA risk, customer impact, and governance exceptions.
- Add richer notification preferences and escalation policies.
- Extend customer portal experiences for external organizations.
- Improve reporting exports and evidence packs.

## Platform Hardening

- Enterprise identity provider integrations.
- Advanced audit review and compliance export.
- More granular data-retention policy support.
- Observability and runtime health dashboards.
- Resilience and backup playbooks.

## AI Roadmap

- More contextual RCA assistance.
- AI-assisted impact analysis for changes.
- Proactive incident trend detection.
- AI-generated knowledge draft review workflows.
- Service health summarization for leadership and customer reporting.

## Integration Roadmap

- Monitoring and observability tools.
- Collaboration and ticketing ecosystems.
- Email and notification platforms.
- CMDB and asset sources.
- Security and event sources.
