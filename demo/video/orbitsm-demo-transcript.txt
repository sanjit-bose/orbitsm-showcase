OrbitSM Demo Video Transcript

01. OrbitSM
Welcome to OrbitSM, an AI enabled enterprise service management and governance platform built for modern, project aware operations.

02. Unified service workspace
OrbitSM brings incidents, service requests, tasks, problems, changes, service level agreements, and executive reporting into one workspace.

03. Project-aware operations
Each project has its own operational context. Dashboards, Kanban boards, tickets, tasks, and SLA signals follow the selected project automatically.

04. Service health dashboards
The dashboard gives leaders and operations teams a live view of workload, SLA posture, backlog, ownership, and service health across the environment.

05. Kanban lifecycle execution
The Kanban view supports lifecycle execution across new, acknowledged, investigating, mitigating, monitoring, resolved, and closed stages.

06. Situation intelligence
Situation intelligence groups related events, highlights active operational patterns, and helps teams move from isolated tickets to service level response.

07. Incident and task execution
Incident and task management support assignment, priority, lifecycle movement, comments, attachments, ownership, requester context, and SLA breach tracking.

08. AI-assisted service intelligence
AI assisted insights help with root cause analysis, similar incident discovery, risk signals, categorization, knowledge base drafts, and KEDB style recommendations.

09. RBAC, ABAC and hybrid governance
Access governance supports RBAC, ABAC, and hybrid models, so organizations can control visibility by role, project, customer account, requester, assignee, hierarchy, and policy attributes.

10. Tenant and admin configuration
Administrators can configure projects, users, customer contacts, SLA policies, email notifications, deployment host and port settings, and the access model used by the tenant.

11. Hardened deployment model
The hardened deployment approach uses HTTPS at the edge, an Nginx reverse proxy, private Docker networking, Gunicorn with Uvicorn workers, PostgreSQL, connection pooling, and backend only secrets.

12. Secure, resilient, extensible
OrbitSM is designed as a secure, resilient, and extensible ITSM platform for enterprise operations, customer service delivery, governance, and AI assisted service intelligence.
